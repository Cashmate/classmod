Why use a class mod? (Click to expand)

  • Overcoming Native Point Restrictions: In vanilla Mordhau, loadout points can only limit weapons to lighter armor by making them expensive—there is no mechanic to enforce heavy armor requirements on heavy weapons. The class mod enforces true archetype roles similar to Chivalry 1.
  • Enabling Underutilized Equipment & Perks: Protection and armor values almost always outweigh utility in vanilla play. When given free rein over point budgets, players consistently prioritize maxing armor over utility. By hard-setting armor baselines, loadouts naturally open up dedicated space for secondaries and perks without sacrificing survivability.
  • Promoting Weapon Variety & Stopping Power Creep: Competitive teams naturally converge on singular "meta" weapons (typically consistent 2HTK options). The traditional "fix" is buffing 3HTK weapons until they compete, leading to aggressive overall power creep. Class-based restrictions isolate weapon balance within specific armor brackets, encouraging diverse team compositions without inflating average lethality.
  • Consistent Time-to-Kill (HTK): When players min-max hybrid setups (like 1-3-2), developers are forced to inflate damage against light armor just to keep heavy armor in check. This creates volatile, frustrating combat where "light" players either get instakilled (headshot) or tank the same damage as a "heavy" player would (bodyshot). Fixed class loadouts keep damage scaling predictable and fair.
  • Unified Hitboxes: Body and Leg damage are unified into a single multiplier, streamlining damage calculation and keeping combat focused on headshots and body hits.
  • Cashmod Foundation: Built on Cashmod as a baseline, with tweaks specifically made with classes in mind.
